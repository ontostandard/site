Try an example:

Built on the fully open OLMo-2 7B. On 60 fresh real claims collected after training it answered 50 correctly; it caught 17 of 20 claims that lack the deciding number and said so wrongly on 1 of 40. It still calls some ordinary solar panels impossible. It is a narrow judge for physical limits of devices, not a general assistant. The model runs at full precision on our own machine. An answer takes about 10 seconds, and the model may be offline at times. Up to 20 checks an hour.
