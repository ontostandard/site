Models know more than they say.

On 201 real claims from ads and press releases, models catch what is physically impossible, but rarely say not enough data when that is the honest answer. We measure that gap and train the knowledge into the answer.

An ablation showed the model learned its own readout in the top blocks rather than reading the probe direction, so we call it a learned readout.

What we got wrong

Results we withdrew.

42 to 47 of 47

We reported that our library took a frontier model from 42 to 47. A clean rerun gave 25 to 27 of 29, p = 0.625.

Reflex and mirror

Earlier fine-tunes taught refusing everywhere or agreeing with lies. An outside audit traced it to one-sided labels and a reward where not enough data was the cheapest answer.

Reading the signal

We first read the fine-tune as using the probe signal. An ablation did not support it, so we changed the claim.
