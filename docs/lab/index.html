The smallest rung of the size ladder

Goal
Rehearse the whole chain on 3B before the larger models.

2026-10-30

Consulting the limits of physics: accepted

Goal
Put into the weights the habit of routing an impossible physical claim to the limit it breaks, without losing everyday answers.
Measured
Sets of 45 questions, 15 of them impossible; a control set that never decided between attempts; a blind judge on 40 everyday questions against the model before the training.
Result
Set A 13 and 14 of 15; control set 11 and 12 of 15; possible claims wrongly flagged 0 and 1 of 30. Everyday answers −6 / −7 net, inside the bar of −8. Four of the six attempts were not accepted.
Led to
The recipe was frozen and the size ladder was pre-registered.
ONTO:2610.0009
2026-10-30

Multi-step everyday arithmetic stays with the base

Goal
See whether the same training also improves everyday calculations of several steps.
Measured
60 new problems, each reference answer recomputed independently; the program the model writes and its words, both scored.
Result
By its program, the trained 7B solves 30.5 of 60 against 34 untrained. By its words it is 3 above its base: when the program is wrong, the words often recompute it.
Led to
The size ladder measures this first, as its primary measure.
ONTO:2610.0009
2026-10-16

Checking its own words

Goal
A model that checks its own answers against the limits.
Measured
Three fresh sets of 48 questions; untrained Qwen 7B answered, then wrote checks of its own answers.
Result
When a check gave a verdict, it was right 34 of 38 times. It caught 11 of its own 45 impossible claims. The pre-registered bar failed.
Led to
The line was closed; the boundary went into the weights instead.
ONTO:2610.0009
2026-10-12

Instructions in the prompt against training

Goal
Find how much of the habit a prompt alone can buy, and whether size changes it.
Measured
Untrained Qwen 7B, 14B and 32B with the same instructions and manual in the prompt, two passes each.
Result
Going to the source by itself: 0.42, 0.39 and 0.27. The trained 7B, with no instructions: 0.90. The instructions also cost true answers at every size.
Led to
The habit is trained, not prompted. A trained size ladder.
ONTO:2610.0009
2026-10-12

Refusal without a source

Goal
Teach the model to say when a number is missing, with no source to consult.
Measured
840 public maths problems with a key number missing, and 840 solvable twins.
Result
Invention fell from 501 to 49 and 64. Correct answers on the solvable twins fell by 18.3 and 14.3 points. At this price it is not usable.
Led to
A refusal is taught only together with the twin that must be answered.
ONTO:2610.0009
