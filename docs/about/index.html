ONTO · About

An independent institute.

ONTO measures what language models can and cannot do, and publishes each result as a numbered, dated document with its fingerprint.

The institute

Canon, lab, archive.

The canon

The institute's work rests on the closed canon GOLD, collected by its founder over more than four decades. It stays closed.

The lab

For a year and a half the lab has trained and measured open models. Every working session is logged with its verdict, and every result goes to the archive.

The archive

Each result is a numbered, dated document. Published texts are never rewritten; corrections come as new versions. Archive →
