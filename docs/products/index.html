ONTO · Products

Three services, by application.

Each is provided under a written agreement. The terms depend on the model and the scope, not on the result.

Now

Measurement of a model's limits

What
Where a language model stops, measured with the instruments of the atlas: both sides of each behaviour as one measure, against the model's own untrained base, on question sets that stay closed so that they keep measuring.
You get
A report in which each number has its denominator, its comparison and the fingerprint of the record that states it.
For
Providers of models, research labs, and teams that fine-tune a model for one field and need to know what it will and will not do.

Apply for a measurement

By agreement

Shifting the boundary into an open model's weights

What
On an open model, we train the habit of consulting a boundary before answering, and measure the result against the same model untrained, on both sides of each behaviour.
What it moved
On Qwen 7B, answers taken from the source rose from 0.00 to 0.90 / 0.88 of 103 held questions; impossible physical claims routed to their limit rose from 0 to 11 / 12 of 15 on the control set.
What it cost
Everyday answers, judged blind against the same model before the training, lost 6 / 7 of 40 net in the accepted training. Every result is reported with its price.
What stays where
The weights stay with you. The recipe and the closed canon GOLD stay with the institute.

In preparation

Institute conclusion on technical claims

What
A written conclusion of the institute on whether a technical claim breaks a known limit, and by how much.
When
After a test on real documents. Until then it is not offered.
How to apply

Write to us.

Write to council@ontostandard.org with the service in the subject, the model, and what you need to know about it. No prices are published; terms are agreed per application. Before you write, you can check how we measure: Method · Verify.
