Each key is computed by a script from a table of physical limits and device classes that was itself frozen before the claims were collected. The date is the day the key was frozen, as recorded in our preregistration log. After a wave has been used to judge a model, it becomes a development set and is not used as a test again. The keys are available to research teams on request.
